Board & Meetings

How Holiday Village is run

The village is run by residents, for residents. Seven neighbors are elected to the Board of Trustees of the Holiday Village Community Services Association (HVCSA). Their meetings are open, and the notes from every one are below.

Meetings

Come and listen, or have your say

Open meetings are usually held on the second and fourth Tuesday of the month, in the clubhouse. Any resident may attend.

To confirm The days come from the dates on past meeting notes. The office can confirm the day and the start time.

Get involved

Run for the Board, or lend a hand

Trustees are elected by residents each spring. In 2023, 280 ballots were cast, a village record, and one seat was settled by drawing an envelope after a tie.

Want your name on next year's ballot, or to help on a committee? The Architectural Review Board needs volunteers right now.
