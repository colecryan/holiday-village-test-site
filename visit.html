The plain facts

Holiday Village at a glance

A 55+ community

A planned adult community in Mount Laurel, Burlington County, New Jersey.

711 homes

Single-family homes, patio homes and condominiums, built by J.S. Hovnanian & Sons from the mid 1980s.

Close to everything

Near Philadelphia, Atlantic City, the Jersey Shore and plenty of shopping.

Common questions

What people ask before moving here

Who can live here?

Holiday Village is a planned 55+ adult community. The office can explain the exact age rules, which are set out in the village's founding papers.

Read the 55+ community papers
What kinds of homes are there?

711 homes in three kinds: single-family homes, patio homes and condominiums. Condos also have their own condo association.

See the village map
How do I find a home for sale?

Homes here are bought and sold privately, usually through local real estate agents. The village office can answer questions about the community itself.

Ask the office a question
Are there monthly fees?

Ask the office about the current fees for the kind of home you're looking at. Condos also have their own condo association and fees.

Ask the office about fees
I'm helping a parent decide. Can I talk to someone?

Yes. Call the office at (856) 866-1753, Monday to Friday. They can tell you what daily life is like and answer questions for both of you.
