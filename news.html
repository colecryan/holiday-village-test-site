From this month's Villager.

From the Board President

The social season is here, and space is limited

Bruce Sleister: "Once again, the social season has begun." The Social and Health & Welfare committees have several winter events planned, and seats go fast. New residents, watch for the sign-up days so you don't miss out.

The Board is also thinking about refreshing the clubhouse front porch and hallway. Your ideas are welcome: send them to the office.

Before the first snow

What the village clears, and what you clear

  • After 3 inches or more, the village's contractor clears driveways, aprons, walkways and sidewalks of single-family and court homes, starting within about two hours of the storm ending.
  • Ice is yours to handle. Calcium chloride pellets are best; rock salt damages concrete.
  • Bring in decorations, and don't park on your lawn or at the clubhouse during a storm.
  • Streets are plowed by Mount Laurel Township: Public Works, 856-234-2070.
Condo corner

New HOA fees start in January

Please check you are paying the new amount. Automatic payment from your bank (ACH) stops late fees; the form is on TownSq, the condo app.

Also: salt buckets are coming (use sparingly), keep your unit number visible for emergency crews, and nothing hung on the new railings.

Thank you

The Health & Welfare team

Marie Campbell thanks every member of the committee she chaired for the last few years: "You guys are the best of the best!" Robin Fields becomes chair in January. Marie: "I am going nowhere; I am just relaxing!"

Outreach

Caring for the caregivers

November's evening with speaker Darlene Arroyo went so well that neighbors are starting a support group. If you care for someone, or expect to, ask the office for the notes and the group.

Out and about

A day in Bordentown

Barbara Fetty walks where Thomas Paine, Joseph Bonaparte and Clara Barton lived, then has fresh pasta at Angelo's Trattoria. Twenty minutes up Route 295. Read it in the December Villager.
