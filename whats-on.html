Ways to give

Neighbors helping neighbors

Toys for Tots

Until Thursday, December 10. New, unwrapped toys in the box off the Clubhouse lobby. No toy weapons and no food.

Warm clothes drive

From January 1. Hats, gloves, coats, pants, shirts and socks, new or gently used, for men and women without homes. Leave them in the Clubhouse lobby.

Caregiver support group

Started by neighbors after November's Caring for the Caregiver evening. Ask the office to put you in touch.

Clubs to join

Find your people

Every club is run by residents. To join, just turn up, or call the office and they will pass your name on.

Books & Friends Evening Book Club

One book a month and a good talk about it. Celebrating its first year this December.

Social Committee

Runs the big nights: trivia, pizza nights, the food truck, cheesesteak day and the barbecue at the pool.

Health & Welfare Committee

CPR classes and other health events. Robin Fields takes over as chair in January.

Women's Craft Group

Seasonal craft mornings. Supplies list sent ahead; the village covers the basics.

Village Travel Club

"Villagers doing travel for villagers": dinners in the clubhouse and group trips planned together.

Outreach Committee

Toy and clothing drives, and caregiver evenings. Always glad of another pair of hands.

Volunteers wanted right now: the Architectural Review Board (the committee that approves changes to the outside of homes) and a Calendar Editor for The Villager. Tell the office you can help.
