Trash and recycling

The answer most people come looking for.

Trash: every Tuesday

Recycling: every other Tuesday.

Out after 4 p.m.

Put cans at the curb no earlier than 4 p.m. the night before, and bring them in by the end of pickup day. Cans live in your garage between pickups.

Big items

Furniture and bedding can go out with regular trash. Your own building debris too: wood cut to 4 feet or less, nails removed. Contractors take their own debris away.
