Your first few weeks

Six steps to feeling at home

  1. Stop by the office and say hello

    1 Holiday Blvd. Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday 9 to 3, Thursday 9 to 4. They will put a name to your face and answer anything.

  2. Get your clubhouse key fob working

    The clubhouse is open every day from 6 a.m. to 11 p.m., and your fob opens the door. Ask the office to activate it.

  3. Give the office your phone and email

    So you hear about events, snow plans and anything urgent. You can also do this yourself on My Info.

  4. Read the welcome packet

    A short guide to how things work here. Open the welcome packet (PDF)

  5. Know your trash day

    Trash every Tuesday, recycling every other Tuesday. Cans out after 4 p.m. the night before. More on trash and recycling

  6. Try one thing this month

    Bingo, chair yoga, the book club, trivia night. Pick one. Popular events fill up, so watch for the sign-up day. See what's on

Who to call

The right number, first time

Most things

The Village office: (856) 866-1753. Key fobs, the clubhouse, events, homes and grounds.

Condo owners

The Associa condo office: 888-884-8490. Condo fees and anything about your building.

Snow on the streets

Mount Laurel Public Works: 856-234-2070. The township plows the roads.

In an emergency, call 911. The village office can't respond to medical needs.

About the village

Holiday Village at a glance

  • A 55+ active adult community built by J.S. Hovnanian & Sons in the mid 1980s
  • 711 homes: single-family, patio homes and condominiums
  • In Mount Laurel, Burlington County, close to Philadelphia, Atlantic City, shopping and the Jersey Shore
  • Clubhouse, pool, pickleball and tennis courts, library, ballroom, billiards and a pond walk
